Core Progression Architecture

🪙 Credits Currency

Platform internal currency. Can be earned & spent. Spending credits NEVER reduces your rank!

⭐ XP Lifetime

Represents cumulative lifetime learning effort. XP never decreases even if competitive rank drops.

🏆 RR (Rank Rating) Competitive

RR controls your competitive rank tier. The leaderboard score also includes verified wins, activity, XP, and credits, so meaningful progress can move your leaderboard position.

RR Earning & Loss Rules

How to Earn RR:

Complete a Lesson Quiz +5 RR
Win a Skill Challenge +20 RR
Solve a Coding Challenge +15 RR
Win Team Race Arena +25 RR
Successful Live Learning Session +15 RR
Contribute Verified Notes +10 RR

When RR is Reduced:

Team Race Defeat -15 RR

No RR loss for watching lectures, opening notes, or normal inactivity.

Leaderboard score = RR + (25 × verified wins) + (5 × activity) + (XP ÷ 10) + (credits ÷ 10). RR alone determines the rank tier.

Promotion & Protection

Automatic Promotion: Reaching the exact RR threshold triggers an instant division promotion (e.g., Gold II → Gold I) and celebration.

Rank Protection Buffer: Upon reaching a major tier (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Master), learners receive 1 Rank Protection Buffer. If a loss would otherwise drop you to the lower tier, the protection is consumed once to preserve your rank.

Leaderboard scoring: Every learner is ordered by the combined leaderboard score: RR + verified wins, activity, XP, and credits using the weights shown above. A stable profile ID is used only when the final score is equal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does spending credits reduce my rank? ↓

Spending credits never reduces your RR. Credits are used for notes and sessions, and your remaining balance only matters as a final tie-breaker when other leaderboard values are identical.

Does XP decrease when I lose a challenge? ↓

No. XP represents lifetime learning effort and knowledge accumulated. XP only increases.

Can I manipulate my RR from frontend? ↓

No. Every RR change requires an atomic ledger entry with idempotency key verification on the server. Client-side numbers are read-only displays.
