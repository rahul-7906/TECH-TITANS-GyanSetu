Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, transparent answers about accounts, credits, micro-sessions, and security.

GyanSetu is an authentic campus learning community. Logging in ensures verified peer profiles, protects your credit ledger balance, saves your learning progress, and prevents spam.

No real money is required! GyanSetu operates on a campus peer credit economy. Every registered student receives 100 bonus credits on signup. You earn additional credits by teaching peers, solving arena challenges, or contributing verified notes.

When you book a 1-on-1 micro-session, the required credits are placed into automated Escrow Protection. They are only transferred to your peer mentor when you click "Mark Doubt Solved". If the peer doesn't show up or your doubt isn't addressed, your credits are refunded in full.

Research shows that students retain the most when tackling single, bite-sized doubts rather than sitting through hour-long lectures. The 15-minute format respects both peers' time, maintains laser focus, and makes asking for help friction-free.

The Speed Race Arena allows campus peers to race in real-time by answering procedural coding questions across 14 academic disciplines. Winning races earns you Campus XP and moves your squad up the department leaderboard.
