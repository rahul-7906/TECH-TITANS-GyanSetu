Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, transparent answers about accounts, credits, and live sessions.

Q: Why do I need to sign in to access features?

GyanSetu is an authentic campus learning community. Logging in ensures verified peer profiles, protects your credit ledger balance, saves your learning progress, and prevents spam.

Q: Do I have to pay real money?

No real money is required! GyanSetu operates on a campus peer credit economy. Every registered student receives 100 bonus credits on signup. You earn additional credits by teaching peers, solving arena challenges, or sharing verified study materials.

Q: What happens to my credits during a 1-on-1 session?

Credits stay safe in Escrow Protection. They are only released to your peer mentor when you confirm that your doubt was clearly and satisfactorily resolved.

Q: What features unlock after signing in?

Once signed in, your personal Dashboard gives you full access to Smart Match (find and book mentors), Live Collab Rooms (shared code editor, whiteboard, voice call), Campus Arena (coding races and 14-domain procedural quizzes), and the Campus Leaderboard.
